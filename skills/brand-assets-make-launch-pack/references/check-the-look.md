# Check The Look

Choose three approved examples:

1. one that has the closest meaning;
2. one that has the closest materials and finish;
3. one that contains the hardest special part, such as a person, hand, connector, or shadow.

Compare at thumbnail size and full size.

Check the silhouette, materials, lines, edges, texture, colors, shadows, layout, crop, background, and accidental text. The result fails when one important visible part looks like it came from a different brand.
