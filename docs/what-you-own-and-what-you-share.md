# What You Own And What You Share

You bring your own examples and whatever rights you have to use them. The package records what you said; it does not decide copyright or trademark law.

- Keep customer files out of the product repo.
- “Look only” means do not copy the artwork.
- Making an image does not give permission to publish it.
- Keep private links, keys, and personal paths out of releases.
- Mark uncertain work `needs review`.
