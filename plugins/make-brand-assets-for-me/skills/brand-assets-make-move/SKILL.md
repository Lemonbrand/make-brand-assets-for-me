# Make A Brand Asset Move

Follow `SHOW → PLAN → MAKE → CHECK → SAVE`.

Read `references/five-steps.md`, `references/ask-the-user.md`, and `references/check-the-look.md`.

## What to do

1. Name the approved source PNG, its job, target size, and requested delivery.
2. When the motion's meaning or delivery is unclear, use the structured ask-user function to choose a small loop, first and last frames, or both. Use one short chat question when the function is unavailable.
3. Make a layer plan for every moving part, contact point, pivot, and shadow.
4. Start from the actual approved pixels. Split, mask, crop, or extend them. Do not redraw the source as a cleaner but different vector.
5. Repair only areas revealed by movement.
6. Keep each moving part and its close shadow separate. Keep texture fixed inside the moving part.
7. Use small motion that explains the asset's job.
8. Check the rest, middle, contact, end, and loop seam frames.
9. Save the requested media, layers, first and last frames, contact sheet, and motion record.

## Finish with

- source path and motion job;
- chosen motion route;
- media and layer paths;
- contact-sheet and record paths;
- plain pass, draft, or needs-review result.
